The licensing portal now lets a business renew, pay and download its licence in a single visit. The work took nine months and replaced three older systems.

What changed

Renewals used to need a counter visit and a printed form. The new service checks the business registration number against the national registry, fills in what the government already knows and asks only for what has changed.

  • One form in place of three
  • Payment by FPX, card or e-wallet
  • A digital licence with a QR code that officers can scan

A renewal that took half a day at the counter now takes about six minutes on a phone.

Who can use it

Any business registered in Malaysia with an active licence. Businesses with an expired licence need to book a counter visit first.
